## Short description

The Golfery is Marin's social sports club in San Rafael, with five premium simulator bays, a PuttView green, a self-pour tap wall, and a coworking lounge, six minutes from Stonetree. Bay Club members get their first session free and 20% off a Golfery membership with code BayClub20.

## Code

BayClub20

## How to redeem

1. Go to golfery.com/bayclub and tap Book your free session.
2. Create a new account in our booking app, then pick a bay, day, and time.
3. On the booking summary screen, tap Apply Promo Code, enter BayClub20 exactly as written, and tap Apply.
4. Confirm the total updated, then book. Bring your Bay Club membership card to check-in.
To join as a member at 20% off, mention BayClub20 when signing up at the front desk.

## Small print

Valid for current Bay Club members who are new to The Golfery. One free session per member; on a 5-session pass, the first session is free. Membership discount applies to the first month of any tier. Offer valid October 1 through December 31, 2026. Cannot be combined with other offers. Subject to bay availability. The Golfery, 647 Irwin Street, San Rafael, CA 94901.
