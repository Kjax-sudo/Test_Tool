Using the logo

  • Use the green logo on light backgrounds and the Flagpole White logo on dark ones and on photos.
  • Leave clear space around the logo about the height of the G in "Golfery".
  • Please do not recolor, stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows. Never retype the name in a script font.
  • The logo with "Simulator lounge / Marin California" underneath is the one to use where people may not know us yet.
